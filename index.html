MotionMIX: An online toolbox for parameterized body motion manipulation

Introduction

MotionMIX is an online toolbox for importing, manipulating, and exporting naturally recorded human body motion. Users can import skeletal animations in GLB, GLTF, or FBX format, or point light display (PLD) data in CSV or TXT format, which MotionMIX automatically converts into a common bone-level representation. Motion can then be edited for individual bones or bone groups by adjusting movement amplitude, average posture, frequency ranges, and phase. Results can be exported as animated GLB files, PLD CSV data, or customizable WEBM videos, with batch processing available for multiple parameter and camera settings.

Parameterized manipulation of bone motion continua

Manipulation of dynamic motion component (e.g. arm swing amplitude)

Manipulation of postural motion component (e.g. average value of arm elevation)

Easy-to-use parameter input interface

Generalizable support for multi-format motion inputs
